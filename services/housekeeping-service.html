SERVICE OVERVIEW

Steady Household Upkeep Tailored to Your Residence

A harmonious home relies on regular, thoughtful maintenance. Helper Home coordinates dependable housekeeping personnel who take responsibility for daily cleaning, dusting, floor care, kitchen hygiene, and general household organization.

Whether you require morning support, full-day domestic care, or a live-in arrangement, we ensure candidate verification and clear mutual alignment from the start.

Important Note: Specific daily duties and timings are aligned directly to your household requirements during initial coordination.
SCOPE & INCLUSIONS

What Housekeeping Service Includes

Our housekeeping support focuses on daily cleanliness, visual order, and functional home maintenance:

01

Daily Floor Care

Sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping of all living areas, bedrooms, hallways, and balconies.

02

Dusting & Surfaces

Regular dusting of furniture, tabletops, consoles, shelves, decor items, and window frames.

03

Kitchen Cleaning

Wiping cooking counters, stovetops, sinks, and exterior appliance surfaces after meals.

04

Utensil Care

Washing, sanitizing, drying, and neatly arranging daily dinnerware, utensils, and glassware.

05

Home Organization

Making beds, straightening cushions, folding blankets, and maintaining order across rooms.

06

Laundry Assistance

Washing machine cycles, hanging laundry to dry, and neat folding as part of the daily schedule.

07

Waste Clearance

Daily trash bin clearing, liner replacement, and waste segregation according to society norms.

08

Household Upkeep

General upkeep that keeps your residence peaceful, clean, and welcoming around the clock.
