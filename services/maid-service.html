SERVICE OVERVIEW

Thorough Housekeeping Structured to Your Schedule

Maintaining a modern apartment or bungalow requires consistent, daily attention. From sweeping living room floors and scrubbing kitchen tiles to washing everyday utensils, Helper Home coordinates maids who understand proper home hygiene and respectful etiquette. SCOPE & RESPONSIBILITIES

What Maid Service Can Include

Depending on your household needs (morning visit, part-time, or full-day schedule), the scope can be customized across the following domestic areas:

01

Floor Care

Daily sweeping, vacuuming, and mopping of bedrooms, living rooms, corridors, and balconies.

02

Dusting & Surfaces

Dusting furniture, tables, bookshelves, entertainment consoles, window frames, and decorative items.

03

Utensil Cleaning

Scrubbing, washing, sanitizing, and neatly stacking daily pots, pans, cutlery, and cookware.

04

Kitchen Assistance

Wiping down gas stoves, kitchen countertops, backsplashes, and daily sink sanitation.

05

Laundry Assistance

Loading and unloading washing machines, hanging clothes to dry, and basic folding as agreed.

06

Household Organisation

Making beds, aligning sofa cushions, tidying up newspapers, and maintaining order.

07

Waste Disposal

Clearing daily waste bins, replacing liners, and segregating household dry/wet refuse.

08

Routine Support

Supporting your family's preferred domestic cadence for consistent daily peace of mind.
