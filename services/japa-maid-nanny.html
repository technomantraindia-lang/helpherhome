POSTPARTUM CARE AT HOME

Nurturing Support for Mother & Newborn Comfort

Transitioning home with a new baby is an emotional and physically demanding period. A caring Japa nanny provides steady assistance with swaddling, baby bath routines, feeding schedule reminders, and keeping nursery linens sanitized so mothers can recuperate with peace of mind.

Medical Care Disclaimer: Japa maid and nanny assistance provides non-medical daily routine support and comfort. Any clinical, pediatric, or obstetrical care requires appropriately qualified healthcare professionals.
DAILY POSTPARTUM SUPPORT

Non-Medical Care Areas Included

Tailored according to mother and family preferences during the early postnatal transition:

01

Newborn Routine Support

Assisting with gentle soothing, swaddling, burping after feeds, and monitoring infant sleep safety.

02

Baby Hygiene Assistance

Warm water baby sponge baths or tub baths, diaper changes, and gentle skin moisturizing routines.

03

Feeding Assistance

Assisting the mother with comfortable feeding positions and bottle sanitization following parental instructions.

04

Mother Rest & Support

Taking attentive care of the infant so the recovering mother can rest, shower, and sleep undisturbed.

05

Baby-Related Organisation

Washing, sterilizing, and neatly arranging baby clothes, bibs, blankets, and crib sheets.

06

Light Baby-Related Home Help

Keeping the nursery tidy, sanitizing bottle warmers, and ensuring a calm, peaceful atmosphere.
