TWO-PERSON COORDINATION

Synergistic Management for Larger Households

Our Domestic Couple Service connects households with two domestic personnel who divide and coordinate household responsibilities collaboratively. One partner often oversees cooking, pantry organization, and interior housekeeping, while the other manages exterior upkeep, property maintenance, errands, or driving.

Agreed Terms: The exact division of labour, accommodation parameters (if live-in), and scope of duties are documented and agreed upon during coordination.
